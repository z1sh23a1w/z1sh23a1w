Common Questions

How do I join the ALSTAR programme?

Joining is simple—no interviews required! Just fill out the registration form on the ALSTAR page. Once registered, you will receive a confirmation email containing everything you need to get started, including a link to join our official MS Teams group.

What exactly is the ALSTAR journey like?

Your journey begins with your 'ALSTAR First Event.' You will be grouped with fellow ALSTARs to plan and execute an event based on a specific theme—ranging from sports festivals and workshops to game shows. It is a hands-on preview of the ALSCO experience.

How do I join ALSCO after being an ALSTAR?

After completing your first event, you can determine if ALSCO is the right fit. If interested, you submit an application form and undergo an interview process. Successful candidates will be invited to join the next ALSCO committee.
